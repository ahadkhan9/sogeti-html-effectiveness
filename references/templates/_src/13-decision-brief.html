Decision brief / Phase 2

Approve the boundary. Fund the proof.

Owner: Chief Claims Officer Prepared by: Sogeti client partner 14 November 2026 Decision needed by: 21 November 2026

Decision requested

Approve the phase 2 strangler boundary and the claims intake slice.

This is a boundary decision, not a platform decision. It authorizes one production slice, one service owner and a go/no-go at week 12. Payments and correspondence remain out of scope.

1. Context

Northline’s claims operation runs on a batch-bound monolith. Every change is a full-book event, and the platform team is fully allocated to regulatory and keep-the-lights work. The business waits; the queue grows; the workarounds become the process.

Discovery tested three options against six criteria. Partnering with Sogeti scored highest on the balanced weighting (80/100), ahead of buying a platform (65) and building in-house (62). The margin is not decisive on its own; the two highest-weighted criteria, time to value and delivery confidence, are the ones the first slice is designed to test.

3. Recommendation

Approve the phase 2 boundary and fund the 90-day discovery with Sogeti. The recommendation is conditional on three commitments, each with a date and an owner.

  1. Name the intake service owner by 21 November. The owner signs the runbook and holds the escalation path. Without a name, the slice is not authorized to go live.
  2. Run the rollback rehearsal with risk by 28 November. The second rehearsal is the evidence risk asked for. It is a condition of the week 12 gate, not a parallel workstream.
  3. Bring the phase 3 case to the week 12 gate. Scale is a separate decision. The gate decides whether the evidence supports another slice, not whether the programme continues.

What would make us recommend stopping

If the slice cannot run live for four weeks with a named owner, a tested rollback and one operating metric that moved, the evidence does not support scale. We will recommend stopping and retiring the dual run.

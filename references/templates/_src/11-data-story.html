02 / What it means

Capability is ahead of ownership. Ownership is ahead of measurement.

The ordering matters. Teams learn to build before they learn to run. Then they learn to run before they learn to prove. The 90-day proof is a way to compress that sequence.

  1. Start with the owner, not the model.A named owner changes the design conversation. It makes the queue, the exception and the rollback part of the brief.
  2. Put the metric in the room.An operating metric is a governance device. It gives the service a reason to exist beyond the demo.
  3. Test the switch before the scale.Rollback is the cheapest risk control available. It is also the one most often left to a document.
  4. Treat scale as a series of services.Programmes scale ambition. Services scale evidence. The second is slower and more durable.

Method and limits

Online survey of 612 directors and above across nine markets, fielded September–October 2026. Self-reported; sample skews toward organizations with an existing AI program. Percentages are rounded. This is an illustrative dataset for the reference, not a published Sogeti study.

What we asked

  • Which AI use cases are live in production today?
  • Who is accountable for the service after go-live?
  • Which operating metric is reviewed monthly?
  • Has the rollback been tested with live-like traffic?
# The handover gap — key findings - 68% have a pilot in production; only 31% have a named service owner. - 22% track an operating metric beyond model accuracy. - 17% have tested a rollback with live-like traffic. - Top barriers: ownership 41%, data contracts 37%, risk and rollback 34%, measurement 29%, funding 21%. - Capability is ahead of ownership; ownership is ahead of measurement.

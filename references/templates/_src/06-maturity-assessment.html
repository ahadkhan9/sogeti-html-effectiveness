02 / What the profile says

Three moves, in order.

The recommendation engine is deliberately simple: the lowest scores first, because that is where the next service will fail.

    How to use this in a real conversation

    Ask each owner for the evidence behind their score. A “3” with no named owner, tested rollback or operating metric is a “2” with better slides. The gap between the score and the evidence is the work.

    This diagnostic is a discussion tool, not an audit or certification. Scores reflect the evidence available on the day. Do not use it to rank teams or individuals.

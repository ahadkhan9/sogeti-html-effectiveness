Workshop synthesis / Northline discovery

What we heard, what it means, what we decided.

Six themes from two days with claims operations, platform, risk and finance. Each one has a quote, an interpretation and a decision. The interpretation is ours; the decision is yours.

Workshop 1 of 2 5–6 November 2026 Amsterdam 18 participants
Purpose

Agree the first slice, the operating constraints and what “done” means for the 90-day discovery.

Method

Six structured conversations, one journey map, one decision wall. No slides after lunch.

Output

Six themes, four decisions, five actions and one unresolved tension for the next session.

6 of 6 selected
01

The queue is the product

“We don’t lose claims. We lose the day.”

What it means

The exception queue is the real service. Automation that ignores it moves work around rather than out. The operating metric is queue age, not model accuracy.

Decision

Instrument the exception queue first. Fund two analysts for the proof period.

02

Batch is a business rhythm

“Finance closes when the file lands.”

What it means

Event-driven does not mean real-time everywhere. Payment and correspondence windows are commitments to finance and customers. Preserve them while decoupling the services behind them.

Decision

Keep the existing finance window in phase 2. Move the scheduler behind an interface.

03

Nobody owns the handoff

“Everyone thinks someone else checks it.”

What it means

The handoffs between intake, assessment and payments are where errors concentrate. A RACI for the current flow does not exist, so the exception path is informal.

Decision

Name a service owner for intake before the first slice goes live. Publish the exception RACI.

04

The workaround is the requirement

“The spreadsheet is our real workflow.”

What it means

Shadow processes encode rules the system never captured. They are evidence, not resistance. Study them before automating anything.

Decision

Add a shadow-process walkthrough to discovery. Log every spreadsheet rule as a candidate requirement.

05

Risk wants a switch

“Show me how you turn it off.”

What it means

Risk will support the slice if the rollback is real and tested. A written fallback is not enough; they want to see it work with live traffic.

Decision

Test the rollback twice before go-live. Invite risk to the second rehearsal.

06

Training is not a change plan

“We learned it in a webinar.”

What it means

Capability is built in the flow of work, not in a session. The team needs coaching at the queue, not another module in the LMS.

Decision

Embed a coach in the first four weeks of live operation. Measure time-to-confidence, not attendance.

What we did not resolve

Who owns the customer communication when an exception crosses the intake and payments boundary. The group agreed to bring a proposal to workshop 2 rather than decide in the room.

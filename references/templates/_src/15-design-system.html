01 / Colour

One blue field. One accent. A lot of grey.

Vibrant Blue is a graphics colour. Red is an accent, not a field. Small red text uses the darker functional red.

03 / Shape

A field with a scooped corner, not a rounded card.

One primary shape per page. Flip it, pin it, keep 5% margins, never rotate it 90°.

05 / Rules

What makes it Sogeti, and what makes it slop.

The gate is subtraction. Every pattern below is legitimate with a job; the defect is the missing job.

Sogeti

  • One Capgemini Blue field per page; grey and white elsewhere.
  • One mono display claim, sentence case.
  • One primary shape, pinned, with 5% margins.
  • Flat surfaces, hairline rules, no wide shadows.
  • A real number with a source and a date.
  • One oversized moment, then quiet.
  • Motion with a job, removed under reduced motion.
  • Export at the end of an interactive artifact.

Slop

  • Blue hero, blue section, blue cards, blue footer.
  • Gradient hero text and a purple glow.
  • Isometric decoration with no referent.
  • Rounded-square icon tiles above every heading.
  • Three equal feature cards because the page felt empty.
  • A buzzword layer with no mechanism behind it.
  • Fade-up on every section; hover-scale on everything.
  • A fake dashboard with invented metrics.

The 2026 overcorrection

Cream, amber and an oversized italic serif is now as reflexive as purple was. Sogeti is a blue, mono, precise brand. Do not sand it into a lifestyle magazine to prove you are not generic.
